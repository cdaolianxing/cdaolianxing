Business Services for Western and Global Markets

Our service architecture combines foundational industrial capabilities with mobile product operations for large-scale international execution.

Scope

Complete Service Coverage

1) New Material Technology R&D

Research and development for building new materials, decorative materials, insulation materials, waterproof materials, environmental materials, metal materials, and chemical products excluding hazardous chemicals.

2) Technical Consulting

Professional technical consulting across product systems, project implementation, process optimization, and overseas rollout support.

3) Technology Promotion

Technology promotion services for distributors, engineering contractors, and enterprise partners in Europe and North America.

4) Technology Transfer

Structured technology transfer programs with documentation, implementation guidance, and partner onboarding.

5) Building Energy-Saving Technical Services

Building energy performance optimization, thermal strategy consulting, and low-energy project technical support.

6) Engineering Technical Consulting

Engineering process consulting for planning, execution, quality control, and material-to-construction alignment.

7) Sales of Related Products

Sales of landscaping materials, hardware products, plastic products, sand and gravel, and cement products through compliant channels.

8) Domestic Trade Agency

Agency services for domestic procurement and distribution coordination across partner networks.

9) Import and Export Services

International goods import/export, excluding items prohibited or restricted by national import/export controls.

10) Mobile Management Application Business

App products published on Google Play and Apple App Store, integrated with materials operation scenarios and ad-supported monetization models for scalable overseas business growth.
