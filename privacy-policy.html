1) Scope and Services

This policy applies to website services and mobile applications published on Google Play and Apple App Store by Chengdu Aolianxing New Material Technology Co., Ltd. Services include technology R&D, technical consulting, technology promotion, technology transfer, material sales, engineering consulting, domestic trade agency, import/export (excluding prohibited or restricted goods), and app-based management workflows.

2) Information We Collect

  • Identity and contact data: name, email, company, region, inquiry details.
  • Technical data: IP address, device type, browser, operating system, app version, logs.
  • Identifiers: cookie identifiers, Android Advertising ID (GAID), iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) where permitted.
  • Usage data: app interactions, session events, feature usage analytics, ad interactions.
  • Support data: customer communication records and troubleshooting metadata.

3) How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve services and mobile applications.
  • To process business inquiries and key account cooperation requests.
  • To ensure legal and regulatory compliance in global markets.
  • To support app distribution, diagnostics, anti-abuse, and fraud prevention.
  • To operate compliant ad monetization: app open ads, rewarded video ads, interstitial ads, and banner ads.

4) Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR Art.6)

  • Consent: marketing, non-essential cookies, and personalized advertising where required.
  • Contract: account services, business communication, and app functionality.
  • Legal obligation: accounting, tax, legal reporting, compliance controls.
  • Legitimate interests: service security, quality improvement, and operational analytics.

5) Age Requirements

  • General minimum age: 13+.
  • EU/EEA: 16+ for consent-based data processing (GDPR Art.8), unless local law permits a lower age with guardian consent.
  • South Korea: 14+.
  • COPPA: no knowing collection of personal data from children under 13.

6) Regional Privacy Rights and Policy Adaptation

GDPR (EU/EEA)

Rights include access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and complaint to supervisory authority.

CCPA/CPRA (California, US)

Rights include know, delete, correct, opt-out of sale/sharing, limit use of sensitive information, and non-discrimination.

COPPA (US)

We do not knowingly collect from children under 13. Parent/guardian requests are handled via support@cdaolianxing.com.

PIPL (China)

We process personal information under legality, legitimacy, necessity, and good faith principles and provide access/correction/deletion rights.

Other Frameworks

  • PDPA (Thailand)
  • LGPD (Brazil)
  • PIPA (South Korea)
  • PDPB/DPDP framework adaptation (India)
  • APEC Privacy Framework

7) App Store and Mobile Application Compliance

  • Google Play policy adaptation: User Data policy, Families context handling, ad disclosure, permissions minimization, data safety declaration.
  • Apple App Store adaptation: App Tracking Transparency (ATT), privacy nutrition labels, purpose limitation, age ratings, and SDK disclosures.
  • In-app ad format disclosures: app open ads, rewarded video ads, interstitial ads, and banner ads.

9) International Transfers

Data may be transferred internationally for service operations. Safeguards may include Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC), contractual controls, and equivalent protection measures required by applicable law.
